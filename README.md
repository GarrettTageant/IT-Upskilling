# IT Upskilling/Career Development

This is a project I created to help me stand out in IT both by learning more than what most people would be expected to know with my limited IT experience, and also to help me develop the skills that will accelerate my career growth, with a focus on Networking.

Over several prompts, I asked Claude to do the following:
1. Identify key areas of growth in IT over the next 10 years
2. Determine what bodies of knowledge, skills, and tools are best suited for helping me stand out at the beginning of my IT career, as well as advancing quickly
3. Identify my current knowledge and skills gaps
4. Create a curriculum where I can teach myself outside of normal classwork
5. Generate projects to test my knowledge and document my progress

This is the home of that project, and I will be documenting my journey as I go along.
